Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 26, 2026

This policy explains what information Balance Digital LLC ("Balance Digital", "we", "us") collects through this website (balancedigitalllc.com) and our software products, how we use it, and the choices you have. Our approach is simple: we collect as little as possible and we never sell personal information.

This website

This website is static. It does not use cookies, analytics, advertising or third-party tracking, and it loads no content from other companies. Our hosting and network provider, Cloudflare, processes standard technical data such as IP addresses, browser type and the pages requested in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. This data is handled under Cloudflare's own privacy policy and is not used by us to identify you.

When you email us

If you email us, we receive your email address, name (if included) and the contents of your message. We use this only to reply to you, provide support, and keep a record of our correspondence. We do not add you to a mailing list unless you ask us to.

Pocket RTA

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access so it can measure sound. Audio is analyzed in your phone's memory in real time to draw the analyzer and meters. It is never recorded, saved to storage or transmitted.

Settings stored on your device

The app saves your preferences on your device: display options, your calibration offset and whether Pro has been unlocked. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else.

Purchases

If you buy Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details, name or Apple Account information.

No accounts, ads or tracking

Pocket RTA has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking code. We do not collect personal information through the app.

Bluewake

Bluewake processes audio locally inside your host application. It does not record, upload or transmit your audio, and it contains no analytics or tracking. If Bluewake is sold through a store or payment processor, that provider handles your payment details under its own privacy policy, and we receive only the order information needed to deliver and support your license (such as your name, email address and what you bought).

InvoiceCell

InvoiceCell runs inside your own Google account. Your Toggl Track API token is stored in your Google account's add-on storage, and your time entries and invoices are written to your spreadsheet; we do not copy them to our servers. To manage paid plans and usage limits, our license service receives your verified Google account email address and keeps a count of invoices generated per month. Subscription payments are processed by Stripe, which handles your payment details under its own privacy policy. A product-specific privacy notice will be published with InvoiceCell at launch.

How we share information

We do not sell or rent personal information. We share it only with service providers that help us run our business (such as hosting, email and payment processing), when required by law, or to protect our rights, users or the public.

Retention and security

We keep correspondence and purchase records only as long as needed to provide support, meet legal, tax and accounting obligations, and resolve disputes. We use reasonable safeguards to protect the information we hold, but no method of transmission or storage is completely secure.

Your choices and rights

You may ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you by emailing david@balancedigitalllc.com. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we will honor requests as required.

Children

Our products are not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the date above. If a future version of a product collects new kinds of information, we will update this policy before that change takes effect.
