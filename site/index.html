Why we build

Made by an engineer who still mixes.

Balance Digital was founded by producer and engineer David Troia. As a studio engineer, his credits include records by Down, Godsmack, Mudvayne, Otep, Ugly Kid Joe and 12 Stones, and he has engineered and produced at Balance Productions since 2004.

Every product starts from a problem met on a real session: a delay that should sound great without an hour of tweaking, a quick way to see what a room is doing, less time spent on invoices. We keep our tools focused, honest about what they do, and respectful of your privacy.
