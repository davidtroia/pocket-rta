Coming soon · macOS

Bluewake

Stereo echo + reverb. Eight classic delay modes feed a plate, hall or shimmer reverb, for long, lush repeats that sound good the moment you drop it on a track.

  • Eight echo modes: Tape, Analog, Digital, Multi-Head, Reverse, Ping-Pong, Lo-Fi and Sweep.
  • Built-in reverb: plate, hall or shimmer, in series or parallel with the echoes.
  • Character in the repeats: wow and flutter, drive, tone and low cut inside the feedback loop.
  • Performance controls: tempo sync, tap tempo, ducking and an infinite Hold switch.
  • 18 presets including Long Tape Shimmer, Dotted Eighth Wash, Huge Ping-Pong Shimmer and Analog Cloud.
  • Audio Unit
  • VST3
  • macOS
  • Apple silicon + Intel

Coming soon · iPhone

Pocket RTA

A spectrum analyzer and SPL meter for live sound, in your pocket. Point it at the room, see what the system is doing, and fix it.

  • Real-time analyzer with 1/1, 1/3, 1/6 and 1/12-octave views, peak hold, and A, C and Z weighting.
  • SPL meter with Leq, max, min and a two-minute level history.
  • Waterfall spectrogram and a signal generator (sine, pink and white noise, log sweep).
  • Calibration against a reference meter or acoustic calibrator.
  • Private by design: audio is analyzed on the device and never recorded or sent.

InvoiceCell

Timesheets and invoices for Toggl Track users, built right into Google Sheets.

  • Import time entries from your Toggl Track workspace into a sheet.

  • Your data stays in your spreadsheet. InvoiceCell talks to Toggl Track directly from your Google account.
