Common questions

How do I calibrate the readings?
Open Settings, then Calibration. Hold a calibrator on the phone's microphone (or place a reference sound level meter beside it), switch the source on, and tap Calibrate to reference. The offset is saved on your device. Without calibration, absolute dB values are only approximate.
How accurate is it?
Pocket RTA is not a certified measurement instrument. Phone microphones vary and roll off at very low and very high frequencies, and the analyzer cannot separate 1/3-octave bands below about 40 Hz. It is built for fast, practical decisions, not for compliance measurements.
The app says the microphone is blocked.
On your iPhone open Settings, then Pocket RTA, and turn on Microphone. If another app is using the microphone, close it and try again.
How do I restore my Pro purchase?
Open Settings in the app, find Pocket RTA Pro, and tap Restore purchase. You need to be signed in with the same Apple Account you used to buy it.
What does Pocket RTA do with my audio?
Nothing leaves your phone. Audio is analyzed in memory and never recorded or sent. See the privacy policy.
