Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Pocket RTA does not collect, store or send your data. This page explains exactly what that means.

Pocket RTA (the app)

Microphone

The app asks for microphone access so it can measure sound. Audio is analyzed in your phone's memory in real time to draw the analyzer and meter. It is never recorded, saved to storage or transmitted.

Settings stored on your device

The app saves your preferences on your device: display options, your calibration offset and whether Pro has been unlocked. This information stays on your device and is not sent to us or anyone else.

Purchases

If you buy Pro, the purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your payment details, name or Apple Account information.

No accounts, ads or tracking

Pocket RTA has no accounts, no advertising, no analytics and no third-party tracking code. We do not collect personal information through the app.

This website

This website is static. It does not use cookies, analytics or advertising, and it loads no content from other companies. Our hosting provider may keep standard server logs for security and reliability, such as IP addresses and the pages requested.

If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply to you.

Children

Pocket RTA is not directed at children and collects no personal information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example if a future version of the app collects anything, we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
