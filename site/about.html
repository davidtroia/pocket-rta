Our founder

Balance Digital was founded by producer and engineer David Troia. As a studio engineer, his credits include records by Down, Godsmack, Mudvayne, Otep, Ugly Kid Joe and 12 Stones, and he has engineered and produced at Balance Productions since 2004.

Balance Digital is the next chapter of that work: plugins, measurement tools and utilities designed by a studio engineer for people who record, mix and produce.

What we care about

That work is where our products come from. Bluewake grew out of years of reaching for the same few delay and reverb sounds on guitars, keys and vocals. Pocket RTA is the quick room check David wanted in his own pocket at the console. InvoiceCell came from the less glamorous side of running a studio: turning tracked hours into invoices.

  • Sound first. Presets and defaults should sound good without a manual.
  • Honest tools. We describe what our software does and does not do.
  • Privacy. Our products do not track you, and our apps keep your audio on your device.
